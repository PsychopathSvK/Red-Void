# Red Void Boss Assets

Each boss has two separate files:

- `boss_<name>.png` = standalone boss artwork
- `boss_<name>_hp.gif` = animated HP-bar HUD

The bot automatically selects the correct GIF from the active boss when `/boss` or `/attack` is used.

Bosses:
- THE RED ABYSS
- THE VOID EATER
- THE WATCHER
- THE RED GOD
